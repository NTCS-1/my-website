About Us

Neighborhood Travel and Cargo Services Ltd is a trusted travel and logistics company based in Kano, with services covering Nigeria and International destinations.

We provide fast, affordable and reliable solutions for flight booking (International), Visa assistance & processing, Hajj & Umrah packages, Hotel reservations and Airport pickup & Drop.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, transparent, and professional Hajj, Umrah, and global travel services while strictly complying with Nigerian, Saudi, and international regulations.

Our Vision

To be Nigeria’s leading and most trusted tour operator, recognized for excellence, integrity, and seamless journeys. We aim to be the first choice for travelers worldwide, providing pilgrims with spiritually fulfilling experiences rooted in ease, dignity, and peace of mind.
